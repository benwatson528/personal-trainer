# Jacked & Tan 2.0 — Programme Reference

Source: "Swole at Every Height" (swoleateveryheight.blogspot.com), official
programme write-up by Cody Lefever (GZCL).

## Overview

- **Program length:** 12 weeks (two 6-week blocks)
- **Structure:** Linear block periodization
- **Weekly schedule:** 4 sessions/week, alternating lower/upper
- **Day 5:** Optional (not programmed) — can be accessories or a T1-focused day

## T1 Structure & Progression

Every session: work up to a Rep Max (RM), then drop sets.

### What "TM" means

**TM (Training Max) is the athlete's estimated one-rep max for that lift.** It is a
single number per lift, fixed, and it does not change week to week. Use the
"User's Training Maxes" section at the end of this document.

Two different things get called a percentage in this programme, and the difference
matters:

- **% of TM** — a fixed percentage of the fixed Training Max. Independent of what
  you actually lift for the RM set. Used for Block 1 drop sets and all T2a work.
- **% of RM** — a percentage of the weight you actually lifted for the RM set that
  session. Only meaningful once you have chosen the RM attempt weight. Used for
  Block 2 drop sets.

### RM attempt weight

The RM set is a real set of the prescribed rep count, taken at a weight derived from
TM. To find it, choose the weight whose estimated one-rep max equals the TM:

    RM attempt weight = TM x 30 / (30 + reps)     for rep targets of 2 or more
    RM attempt weight = TM                        for a 1RM (the max IS the TM)

The 1RM case is listed separately because the formula inverts the Epley estimate,
which is unreliable at a single rep — a one-rep set is the maximum itself, so take
the TM directly.

| Rep target | RM attempt weight | Squat (TM 130) | Deadlift (TM 185) |
|------------|-------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 10RM | 75.0% of TM | 97.5kg | 138.75kg |
| 8RM | 78.9% of TM | 102.5kg | 146kg |
| 6RM | 83.3% of TM | 108.5kg | 154kg |
| 5RM | 85.7% of TM | 111.5kg | 158.5kg |
| 4RM | 88.2% of TM | 114.5kg | 163kg |
| 3RM | 90.9% of TM | 118kg | 168kg |
| 2RM | 93.8% of TM | 122kg | 173.5kg |
| 1RM | 100% of TM | 130kg | 185kg |

Round to the nearest loadable weight with the athlete's equipment. A 10RM at
97.5kg is the correct week 1 squat, not 110kg — 110kg for 10 reps implies a
one-rep max well above the training max, which contradicts the TM.

| Week | RM | RM attempt | Drop Sets | Notes |
|------|-----|------------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 10RM | 75% of TM | 70% TM x 6 reps x 3+ sets | Last set optional AMRAP |
| 2 | 8RM | 78.9% of TM | 75% TM x 5 reps x 3+ sets | |
| 3 | 6RM | 83.3% of TM | 80% TM x 4 reps x 3+ sets | |
| 4 | 4RM | 88.2% of TM | 82.5% TM x 3 reps x 3+ sets | |
| 5 | 2RM | 93.8% of TM | 85% TM x 2 reps x 4+ sets | |
| 6 | 1RM | 100% of TM | Rest | No drop sets — 1RM attempt |

Block 1 drop sets are a percentage of TM, **not** of the RM attempt weight. They do
not change when the RM attempt differs from the table.

### Block 2 — Weeks 7-12 (drop sets as % of day's RM)

| Week | RM | Drop Sets | Notes |
|------|-----|-----------|-------|
| 7 | 6RM | 85% of RM x 3 reps x 5+ sets | |
| 8 | 4RM | 85% of RM x 2 reps x 5+ sets | |
| 9 | 2RM | 85% of RM x 1 rep x 5+ sets | |
| 10 | 5RM | 90% of RM x 2 reps x 3+ sets | |
| 11 | 3RM | 90% of RM x 1 rep x 3+ sets | |
| 12 | 1RM | Rest | |

Block 2 drop sets are a percentage of the RM attempt weight chosen that session, so
work the RM attempt first, then calculate the drop sets from it.

**AMRAP expectations:** Block 1: +3 to +5 reps on last set rep-outs. Block 2: +1 to +3 (heavier, less gas).

## T2a Structure & Progression — % of Training Max

**The percentage applies to the TM of that day's T2a lift — not the T1 lift.**
This is the single most common error in this programme. Look up the T2a
exercise in the day's row of the Workout Structure table, then use *that
exercise's* TM.

Worked example, Week 1, Day 1: T2a is `Deadlift (Barbell)`, whose TM is 185.
50% of 185 = **92.5kg**. It is not 65kg — 65kg is 50% of the *squat* TM of 130,
which is the T1 lift that day and has nothing to do with T2a.

| Week | % TM | Reps | Sets |
|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | 50% | 10 | 4 |
| 2 | 60% | 8 | 4 |
| 3 | 70% | 6 | 4 |
| 4 | 75% | 4 | 5 |
| 5 | 80% | 2 | 7 |
| 6 | — | Rest | — |
| 7 | 70% | 6 | 5 |
| 8 | 75% | 5 | 5 |
| 9 | 80% | 4 | 5 |
| 10 | 82.5% | 3 | 6 |
| 11 | 85% | 2 | 7 |
| 12 | — | Rest | — |

T2a TM is recalculated at Week 7 from the Week 6 1RM.

## MRS Work — T2b, T2c and T3 (Accessories)

T2b, T2c and T3 movements all use Max Rep Sets (MRS). Find the programmed RM, then do
2 more max-rep sets at the same weight — 3 sets total per exercise. Rest 30-60s between
sets, and never go to true failure — keep 1-2 reps in the tank.

T2b and T2c share one RM progression; T3 has its own (lighter, higher reps).

| Week | T2b / T2c RM | T3 RM |
|------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 15RM | 20RM |
| 2 | 12RM | 18RM |
| 3 | 10RM | 16RM |
| 4 | 8RM | 14RM |
| 5 | 6RM | 12RM |
| 6 | Rest | 10RM |
| 7 | 15RM | — |
| 8 | 12RM | — |
| 9 | 10RM | — |
| 10 | 6RM | — |
| 11 | Rest | — |
| 12 | Rest | — |

Weeks 7-12 have no new T3 RM: continue MRS progression at the most recent programmed
T3 RM.

## Warmup Sets

- **T1:** up to 3 warmup sets, marked `W` in the Strong CSV, stepping up toward the
  working weight for the day's RM attempt.
- **T2a:** exactly 1 warmup set, marked `W`.
- **T2b, T2c, T3:** no warmup sets. Start at the working weight.

Never add warmup sets after T1 or T2a. Warmup sets rest 60 seconds (see below),
not the tier's working rest.

## Rest Periods

- Warmup sets (any tier): 1 minute (60s)
- T1: 3 minutes (180s)
- T2a: 3 minutes (180s)
- T2b: 2 minutes (120s)
- T2c: 1 minute (60s)
- T3: 1 minute (60s)

## Key Rules

- T1 and T2 movements in the same workout must not match (lift variety required).
- Never go to true failure on MRS work — keep 1-2 reps in the tank.
- Last set of T1 drop sets has an optional AMRAP rep-out.
- T2a is always % of TM; T2b/T2c are always MRS.
- Every T2b, T2c and T3 movement is 3 max-rep sets.
  Do not add a fourth set to work up to true max reps — the extra set is not
  programmed for this athlete.
- This document is the programme definition. It is never silently rewritten.

## Weight Calculation

The AI agent must calculate and populate the exact weight for every exercise in the Strong CSV template. Use the programme reference (Training Max percentages, RM targets) and the user's recent workout history to determine what weight should be used. Do NOT leave weights blank or ask the user to figure out MRS — the agent determines the weight based on what the user has been lifting recently and the programme prescription.
