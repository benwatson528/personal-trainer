# Beginner Pete Plan — Programme Reference

Source: "Beginner Training" (thepeteplan.wordpress.com/beginner-training/),
the official 24-week beginner Pete Plan by the Pete Plan blog.

## Overview

- **Program length:** 24 weeks
- **Weekly schedule:** 3 core sessions/week on separate days, plus 2 optional sessions
- **Structure:** Gradual volume increase with continuous improvement via pace targets

## Session Guidance

### Steady Rows (Day 1 & Day 3)

- Distance increases ~500m/week in the early weeks, then plateaus around 10000m.
- **Key:** Hold the same average pace week to week even as distance increases.
- Focus on technique, relaxation, efficiency — smooth acceleration through the drive, slow and relaxed recovery.
- Don't go faster than target pace, especially at the start.

### Interval Sessions (Day 2)

- Intervals get longer and rest periods vary by week.
- **Key:** Reference the most recent similar session in the logbook and aim for the same or 1-2 seconds faster pace.
- Don't get carried away in early intervals — there are many weeks ahead to increase intensity.

### Optional Sessions (Day 4 & Day 5)

- For the keen trainer — complete core sessions every week first.
- Judge by how you feel and how hard the core sessions were.
- If tired, concentrate on technique and don't worry about pace.
- If feeling good, go 1-2 seconds faster than the steady rows.

## Key Rules

- Complete all 3 core sessions every week on separate days.
- Optional sessions are just that — optional. Rest is important.
